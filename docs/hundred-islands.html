Hundred Islands

Alaminos, Pangasinan

Overview

The Hundred Islands National Park is a stunning archipelago consisting of 124 islands and islets scattered across the Lingayen Gulf in Alaminos City, Pangasinan. This protected marine sanctuary is one of the Philippines' most treasured natural wonders.

Formed through centuries of coral reef growth and geological uplift, these mushroom-shaped islands rise dramatically from turquoise waters, creating a breathtaking seascape that attracts thousands of visitors annually.

The park offers a perfect blend of adventure and relaxation, with activities ranging from island hopping and snorkeling to kayaking and cave exploration. Each island has its own unique character, from Governor's Island with its diving platform to Quezon Island with pristine beaches.

Visitor Information

Best Time to Visit

November to May (dry season) for the best weather and calm seas

How to Get There

5-hour drive from Manila via TPLEX and SCTEX. Buses available from major terminals.

What to Bring

Sunscreen, swimwear, towels, waterproof bags, snorkeling gear, and plenty of water
